Literary Bi☯graphy

Naeem Aziz is

Md. Naeem Aziz is a Bangladeshi poet, author, engineer and researcher, born on 10 December 1998. His literary works have gained national and international recognition. One of his poems, Rule Over Ashes, has been featured in an English Literature textbook for upper secondary education in Italy. He is the recipient of several national and international literary awards and recognitions for excellence in poetry and literature. Writing primarily in English, his works explore themes of humanity, justice, society, history, and emotion. Through poetry, he aims to connect cultures, inspire empathy, and promote global friendship through literature.
